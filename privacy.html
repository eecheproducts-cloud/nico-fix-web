2. What data we use and why

  • WhatsApp chat: your number, your WhatsApp name, and the messages and photos you send. The first replies come from an automated assistant (AI, provided by Anthropic) that asks about the job so Nico can prepare a quote. The AI also helps prepare an internal draft of the quote from your request, notes and photos; Nico reviews it and decides the price. WhatsApp/Meta also processes the conversation under their own privacy policy.
  • Bookings on this site: name, WhatsApp number, address, postcode, type and time of the booking, and optionally a description and photos of the job.
  • Abuse protection: a one-way hash (HMAC) of your IP address, only to limit repeated requests. We never store the IP itself in this record.
  • Managing your job: our internal app keeps your request, the checklist and photos of the visit, the quote, the agenda and a history of who did what on the job. Only the two people who work at Nico Fix can sign in, with a password.
  • Summaries and translations: the full WhatsApp chat is deleted after 14 days without activity. Before that, we make a short summary of your request and the conversation (what you asked for, photos, agreements and times), which we keep as part of the job record. If you write in English or Dutch, we also keep a Spanish translation of your request and your last message, with the same retention period. A person reviews the result; no automated decisions are made about you.
  • Clients from Zoofy: if you come through the Zoofy platform, Zoofy gives us your name, contact details and order number so we can do the job. Zoofy processes your data under its own privacy policy.
  • Proof of acceptance: when you accept a quote on WhatsApp, we keep a screenshot of that message, cropped to what is needed, as proof of the agreement and of your request to start within the 14-day withdrawal period.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics, only if you accept it in the cookie banner.

We use the data only to manage your request or booking and to write to you on WhatsApp about it. No marketing, no selling.

3. Legal basis

  • Name, phone, address, booking details, description and photos: steps taken at your request before a contract (art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Hash of the IP address: our legitimate interest in security and preventing abuse (art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Job record and proof of acceptance: performing the contract (art. 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in proving the agreement and handling warranty claims (art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Invoice data: legal tax obligation (art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Google Analytics: your consent only (art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time with "Cookie settings".

Your phone number and address are required to book, because we need them to reach you and to come to you. The description and photos are optional. Photos showing people, faces or documents are not stored: each photo goes through an automatic check, and photos with people or documents are rejected when the check detects them.

4. Who receives the data

Service providers that process data on our behalf:

  • Google Cloud (database and server functions, region europe-west4, Netherlands) and Google Calendar (agenda; the event title has no personal name, and the event location holds the job address, which is needed for navigation, but no name, phone or description). Our internal app also runs on Google (Firebase: login, database and photo storage in the EU). Google Cloud processes the data in the EU; the login service may process the sign-in emails of our two staff accounts in the USA. Google may transfer data outside the EU under standard contractual clauses (SCC) or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Google Routes API: only postcodes, to calculate travel time.
  • Notion (USA): our job records. Transfer under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses (SCC).
  • Anthropic (USA): the AI behind the WhatsApp assistant, the photo check, the internal quote drafts, and the summaries and translations of your request and chat (including our internal job page). Transfer under SCC.
  • Meta (WhatsApp) and Dualhook: delivery of WhatsApp messages. Both may process data outside the EU, under their own terms and standard contractual clauses (SCC).
  • ntfy (Binwiese LLC, USA): internal notifications to Nico Fix's phones (for example "new visit booked"). They only carry a job number, date, time and city, never your name, phone, address or messages. Your details stay in our systems, which require a login. The service keeps a notification only briefly, to deliver it.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this site and sees the IP address of visitors when the site loads.
  • PDOK (Dutch government): receives only the postcode you type, to check it exists, and the text you type in the address field of the booking form, to suggest addresses. It is not stored.

We don't sell your data and don't share it for anyone's advertising.

Automated assistant

Your first messages on WhatsApp are answered by an automated assistant, which asks about the job so Nico can prepare a quote. Nico reads the conversation and takes it over personally. If you ask for a person, have an urgent problem, or the assistant can't help, it passes your message to Nico right away and stops replying.

If you don't want our messages

If you ask us to stop writing to you (for example "stop"), we keep your phone number and the date on a do-not-contact list, only so we never write to you again, for as long as that request stands. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in respecting your objection (GDPR art. 6(1)(f) and 21). You can still write to us yourself whenever you like.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, erase or restrict the use of your data, to receive it in a portable format, and to object to its use. Write to info@nicofix.nl or on WhatsApp. We answer within 1 month. If you think we are handling your data wrongly, you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens): autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl.

7. Automated decisions

There are no automated decisions with legal effect. The WhatsApp assistant is an AI system, and a person (Nico) confirms every job and every price.

When you book on this site, we use the text you write in the description to choose the language (English or Spanish) of our WhatsApp messages. This happens on our own server; nothing is sent to anyone else for it, and we only keep the language chosen.

8. Postcode check

When you use "Not sure if I cover your area?", the postcode you type is sent to PDOK to check that it exists and where it is. We don't store it and it isn't linked to you.

9. Cookies and local storage

This site stores in your browser (localStorage) only your language choice and your cookie choice, so it remembers them on your next visit. While you book, a private link token is kept in the tab session (sessionStorage) and disappears when you close the tab. This is purely functional and not shared with anyone.

Only if you click "Accept" in the cookie banner, the site uses Google Analytics to count visits and clicks on the WhatsApp buttons. Google Analytics sets the cookies _ga and _ga_<id> (kept up to 2 years) and Google processes this data for us. We never send your name, phone, address or postcode to it. If you click "Decline", no analytics is loaded. You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of the home page.
