2. What data we use, why, and on what basis

  • Your request or booking (name, phone, address, messages, photos, description): to answer you, quote and do the job. Basis: steps at your request before a contract and performing the contract (art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Address and phone are needed to book; description and photos are optional.
  • Our records of the job: to do the job, prove the agreement and handle warranty claims. Basis: contract and legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(b) and (f)).
  • Invoices: legal tax obligation (art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Security of this site: basic technical data to prevent abuse. Basis: legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Messages after a job (for example a review request or a credit you earned): legitimate interest in contacting our own clients, with an easy way to refuse in every message (art. 6(1)(f) and art. 11.7 Telecommunicatiewet).
  • Analytics: Google Analytics, only with your consent (art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time with "Cookie settings".

Some replies on WhatsApp come from an automated assistant. A person from Nico Fix always handles your job and decides the price. We don't sell your data. If you don't want our messages, write "stop" and we will not contact you again.

Google reviews

If you review Nico Fix on Google, we may reply and show your first name, stars and text on our website (art. 6(1)(f)). You can ask us to remove it at any time, without giving a reason; we do it within one month at most.

Recommendations

If you tell us who recommended Nico Fix, or you recommend a friend, we keep that name or credit only to apply the benefit. Conditions: nicofix.nl/referidos. You can ask us to correct or delete it, or object, at any time.

3. Who receives the data

Only service providers that help us run Nico Fix, under processing agreements, and public authorities when the law requires it. The categories are: cloud hosting, database and storage (EU); calendar and record-keeping tools; messaging services; website hosting and maps services; and artificial intelligence and software services. Some of these providers are in the USA. In that case the transfer is protected by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. You can ask us for the names of the providers and a copy of the safeguards.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, erase or restrict the use of your data, to receive it in a portable format, and to object to its use. Write to info@nicofix.nl; we answer within 1 month. If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens): autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl.

6. Cookies

This site stores in your browser only your language and cookie choice. Only if you click "Accept" in the cookie banner, the site uses Google Analytics (cookies _ga and _ga_<id>, up to 2 years) to count visits; we never send it your name, phone or address. If you click "Decline", nothing is loaded. You can change your choice with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of the home page.
