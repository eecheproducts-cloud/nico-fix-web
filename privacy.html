Last updated: November 2026.

1. Who is responsible

Nico Fix, a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) of Nicolás Echechurre, handyman in the Netherlands. KVK: 95562028 · Email: info@nicofix.nl · Address: J A Alberdingk Thijmstraat 69 A, 3117 RE Schiedam.

2. What data we use, why, and on what basis

  • Your request or booking (WhatsApp number and name, messages, address, postcode, photos, description, booking details): to answer you, quote and do the job. Basis: steps at your request before a contract and performing the contract (art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Address and phone are needed to book; description and photos are optional. Photos with people or documents are not stored.
  • Job record (request, visit notes and photos, quote, agenda, history, summaries and, if you write in English or Dutch, a Spanish translation): to do the job and handle warranty claims. Basis: contract and our legitimate interest in proving the agreement (art. 6(1)(b) and (f)). The WhatsApp chat itself is deleted after 14 days without activity; we keep only a short summary. When you accept a quote on WhatsApp we keep a cropped screenshot as proof.
  • Invoices: legal tax obligation (art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Clients from Zoofy: Zoofy gives us your name, contact details and order number to do the job.
  • Abuse protection: a one-way hash of your IP address, kept 2 hours. Basis: our legitimate interest in security (art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Messages after a job (review request, referral programme, credits): legitimate interest in promoting our own services to our clients, with an easy way to refuse in every message (art. 6(1)(f) and art. 11.7 Telecommunicatiewet).
  • Recommendations: if you tell us who recommended us, we keep that name, and if you recommended a friend, a credit on your job. We never share either person's job details with the other. Conditions: nicofix.nl/referidos. Basis: legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Analytics: Google Analytics, only with your consent (art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time with "Cookie settings".

The first WhatsApp replies come from an automated assistant (AI). Nico reads the conversation, takes it over, and confirms every job and price; there are no automated decisions with legal effect. If you ask for a person, the assistant stops replying. We don't sell your data.

If you don't want our messages: write "stop". We then keep your number and the date on a do-not-contact list, only to respect your request (art. 6(1)(f) and 21).

Google reviews

If you review Nico Fix on Google, Google tells us your name, stars and text. We use them to reply and, if you are a client, we note it on your job record for 24 months so we don't ask again. We may show the first name, stars and text of some public reviews on our website (art. 6(1)(f)). You can ask us to remove one at any time, without giving a reason; we do it within one month at most.

Recommendations (referral programme)

See the conditions at nicofix.nl/referidos. We delete the recommender's name 24 months after it is recorded; a used credit stays with the invoice for 7 years (tax). You can ask us to correct or delete it, or object, at any time.

3. Who receives the data

  • Google Cloud, Firebase and Calendar (EU, Netherlands region): database, app, photos and agenda. Google may transfer data outside the EU under standard contractual clauses (SCC) or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Notion (USA): our job records. Transfer under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and SCC.
  • Anthropic (USA): the AI for the assistant, photo check, drafts and summaries. Transfer under SCC.
  • Meta (WhatsApp) and Dualhook: delivery of WhatsApp messages, under their own terms and SCC.
  • Other providers: GitHub Pages (hosts this site and sees visitors' IP address), Google Routes and PDOK (only postcodes or address text, not stored), and Zoofy for jobs that come through it.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, erase or restrict the use of your data, to receive it in a portable format, and to object to its use. Write to info@nicofix.nl or on WhatsApp; we answer within 1 month. If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens): autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl.

6. Cookies and local storage

This site stores in your browser only your language and cookie choice, and a private booking link token while you book. This is functional and shared with no one. Only if you click "Accept" in the cookie banner, the site uses Google Analytics (cookies _ga and _ga_<id>, up to 2 years) to count visits and clicks on the WhatsApp buttons; we never send your name, phone, address or postcode to it. If you click "Decline", nothing is loaded. You can change your choice with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of the home page.
